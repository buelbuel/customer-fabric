# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome good-faith questions and disagreement. Critique ideas and code without personal attacks. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and publication of private customer data or credentials are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation proportionately. Apply decisions consistently, explain them privately where practical, and allow appeal to another maintainer where one is available. Report concerns through repository private reporting channels when available; never post sensitive evidence publicly. Maintainer contact and escalation ownership must be established before public community launch.
